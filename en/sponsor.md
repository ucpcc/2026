## Sponsorship Inquiry

Hello, we are the Union of Clubs for Programing Contests.

The Union of Clubs for Programing Contests is an alliance of 32 clubs from 31 universities gathered to prepare for and participate in domestic and international programming contests. We **host a summer contest (UCPC)** every year with the **goal of fostering interaction and growth within the university student algorithm problem-solving community**.

This year, we are also planning to **host UCPC 2026 during July-August**, and we would like to **request your company's valuable sponsorship** for the successful operation of the contest.

## Sponsorship Areas

- **Essential costs for smooth operation, such as venue rental, server fees, and participant souvenirs**
- **Prizes provided to top-placing teams and participants**
  - If financial sponsorship is difficult, product sponsorship is also possible.
- **Costs for problem setting and reviewing**

## Promotional Benefits

If you decide to sponsor us, the following are possible:

- We can provide a presentation session for each sponsor after the contest ends and before the rankings and editorials are announced.
- We will continuously promote sponsors by displaying your logo on the contest website, problem sheets, editorial sheets, and various promotional materials.
- You can award a special prize under a name desired by the sponsor.
- Problems based on company-related content can be created.
- You can operate a sponsor booth at the Finals.
- We provide individual contact information if participants have agreed to the provision of their personal information.

## How to Sponsor

We ask for your active interest and positive consideration of this sponsorship, which can be expected to have practical effects such as vitalizing the university student algorithm problem-solving community, promoting your company's technology, and recruiting outstanding talent.
