We are calling for tasks to be used in UCPC 2026! We look forward to your creative and interesting problems.

The Call for Tasks for UCPC 2026 will close on April 15, 2026. 
## Submission Method

Please submit both the problem and its editorial (solution).

## Process

1. Please submit the problem materials including the following details via the [Google Form (link)](https://forms.gle/hoHPVrdvuYTE24ii9).
   - The real name and affiliation of the author or authoring team.
   - A summarized problem description and editorial (Drafts are fine!).
     - Please submit it in **PDF format**.
     - A summarized description containing only the minimum elements necessary to solve the problem.
     - An editorial explaining the intended solution of the problem in text.
   - In the editorial, please also include the materials or sources you directly or indirectly referred to, or from which you got the idea.
   - The anti-spam password to enter in the Google Form is `UCPC2026`.
2. The existing judging committee will thoroughly review the submitted problems and reply with whether the problem will be used. Until then, the following are restricted, and if violated, the problem selection will be canceled.
   - **(No Problem Leakage)** Please do not mention the submitted problem or propose the same problem to another contest.
     - If you accidentally leak the problem, please notify the steering committee of the leak as soon as possible.
3. If your problem is selected, please cooperate with the instructions from the organizers. Until the contest ends, the following are restricted:
   - **(Ban on Contest Participation)** You cannot participate in UCPC 2026.
   - **(No Problem Leakage)** You cannot mention the submitted problem anywhere except to the contest steering committee.

## Restrictions

At the discretion of the judging committee, submitted problems may be rejected if they are considered to fall into one of the following categories:

- Well-known problems
  - Problems that simply require printing a message.
  - Problems that simply implement an algorithm -- unless the algorithm is not well-known.
- Problems that do not align with the purpose of the contest
  - Problems where measuring programming ability is not the primary goal.
- Problems lacking originality
  - Problems that have appeared in other contests.

## Notes

- The problems submitted to the UCPC 2026 Call for Tasks will be reviewed by Geunyoung Jang (azberjibiou, KAIST), Jongseo Lee (leejseo, KAIST), Sunghyun Lee (hibye1217, Hanyang University), and Minchurl Shin (fefe, KAIST).
- For efficient review, LLMs may be utilized, but the review will only be conducted in an environment configured so that the submitted materials are not used for model training.
